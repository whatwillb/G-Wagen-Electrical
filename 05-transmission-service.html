Page 5: Transmission service before converter replacement

Once the electrical baseline and transfer-case checks are done, service the 722.6 transmission and retest the same rumble condition. This is the highest-value move before deciding on converter replacement.

What to inspect in the pan

  • Light gray paste on the magnet: usually normal wear
  • Heavy metallic glitter or chips: bad sign
  • Burnt smell or very dark fluid: supports clutch distress and fluid breakdown
Use the correct Mercedes-compatible ATF for the 722.6 and set the fluid level correctly at the proper temperature.

Step-by-step

  1. Drain the transmission fluid.
  2. Remove the pan and inspect the magnet and pan bottom.
  3. Replace the filter and pan seal as needed.
  4. Refill with correct fluid and set level correctly.
  5. Reset transmission adaptations if your tool supports it.
  6. Repeat the exact same road test and live-data capture.
