Page 1: Electrical baseline

Even with a new battery and alternator, bad grounds, poor cable connections, or voltage drop can still trigger undervoltage and CAN faults. Start here.

Target readings

TestGood target
Battery at restAbout 12.6 V after resting
Engine runningAbout 13.7–14.7 V
Ground-side voltage dropIdeally under 0.1–0.2 V on a good path
Positive-side dropVery low and stable under load
Clear all codes only after you inspect and tighten the main electrical connections. Then rescan and note what comes back immediately.

Step-by-step

  1. Fully charge the new battery.
  2. Measure voltage at the battery posts with the engine off.
  3. Measure again at idle, then at 1,500–2,000 rpm, then with lights and blower on.
  4. Disconnect battery negative before cleaning grounds.
  5. Check and clean: battery terminals, main body ground, engine-to-chassis strap, alternator B+ lead, rear-body grounds.
  6. Reconnect, then do voltage-drop tests under load.
  7. Clear all codes and rescan. Write down what returns immediately.
